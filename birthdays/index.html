How much does a birthday photo booth cost?

Birthday packages start at $550 for Mango's Luxury Experience, up to $1,400 for Mango's Big Kahuna Experience, with live keychain and magnet stations available as add-ons. Final pricing depends on hours, backdrop, add-ons, and travel.

Yes. Our experiences are built for any age, from kids' parties to milestone birthdays, with props and templates customized to your theme.

Can Mango the mascot come to the party?

Yes! Mango is available to make a special appearance at your event — just ask when you check your date.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Naples, Marco Island, and the surrounding Southwest Florida region. We travel to your venue.
