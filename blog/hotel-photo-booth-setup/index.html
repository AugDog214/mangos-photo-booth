Where should the photo booth go in a hotel ballroom?

Give the booth a visible spot with room for a group to gather, a clear route past the line, and a place to collect prints. Agree on the spot with the banquet captain before the floor plan goes final.

By Mango’s Photo Booth & Entertainment · November 14, 2026

Draw the line on the floor plan

On a plan of the room, draw three arrows. The first takes a guest from cocktail hour to the camera. The second takes them from the camera to their print. The third takes them back to their table. If the arrows cross the entrance, a server station or the route to the accessible seating, try the other side of the room.

For a Naples hotel reception with drinks in the foyer and dinner in the ballroom, choose the room where you want the booth operating during your booked hours. Ask whether the doors between the two spaces stay open. A booth behind a closed partition can look perfect on paper.

Send one setup note to both teams

Include the floor plan, event date, room name, loading entrance, permitted arrival time, outlet location and the name of the person opening the room. Ask Mango’s to confirm the footprint for your specific backdrop and keepsake station. Leave space to turn and pose together, including for guests using mobility aids.

For an outdoor terrace, get the indoor backup space in writing. Decide who calls the move and when, while the equipment can still be set up before guests arrive.

Give the coordinator a question they can forward

“Can our booth, pickup table and waiting guests fit here while your staff serve dinner? If we need another spot, which room and power outlet can we use?” Include a photo taken from the guest entrance. It shows a doorway or sightline that a floor plan can miss.
